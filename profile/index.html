Section 01 · Chairman’s message

Every door opens
for the right reason.

Merit · Transparency · Fairness · Quality

It gives me great pleasure to present the institutional profile of the Education Testing Council (ETC). ETC was created with one simple belief: every young person in Pakistan deserves an equal chance to show what they know and what they can do. A well-made test is one of the fairest tools a nation has for opening doors to education, scholarships, jobs and professions.

For many students, a single test result can shape the next chapter of their life. That is why we treat every assessment as a serious public trust. Our tests must measure ability and effort, not background, connections or luck. To achieve this, ETC follows standardized procedures, secure examination practices and international good practice in test design, so that the same rules apply to every candidate, in every city.

Over the past few years, ETC has grown into a national assessment platform. It now serves universities, government departments, regulatory bodies and professional councils across the country. Each new partnership reflects growing confidence in our methods, and each one reminds us that confidence must be earned again with every test we deliver.

Looking ahead, our priorities are clear. We will keep strengthening the quality of our questions, the security of our operations and the clarity of our communication with candidates. We will invest in technology, research and people, so that our assessments remain reliable, valid and fair as the needs of the country change.

I am grateful to the Higher Education Commission, the members of the Board of Governors, our partner institutions and the dedicated ETC team for their support. I invite all stakeholders to join us in building a culture where opportunity follows merit, and where every candidate can trust the result they receive.

Section 02 · Executive Director

Strengthening
assessment.

Message from the Executive Director, Higher Education Commission

The Higher Education Commission established the Education Testing Council to give Pakistan a credible, professionally managed and self-sustaining national testing body. Our aim is straightforward: to make sure that access to higher education and professional opportunities is decided by merit, through processes that are open, consistent and trusted.

Assessment in the modern world is changing quickly. Institutions need tests that are standardized, secure and quick to report, while candidates need clear information and fair treatment. ETC responds to these needs by combining sound measurement methods with appropriate technology. Every stage, from writing a question to publishing a result, is guided by evidence and checked for quality.

A central priority is public and institutional confidence. This depends on good governance, clear procedures, secure examination arrangements and timely results. ETC will continue to build its professional capacity so that every assessment is delivered with impartiality and accountability.

Looking forward, ETC will expand its support for admissions, scholarships, recruitment and professional licensing. It will deepen collaboration with universities, public-sector organizations, employers and professional bodies. Through careful use of digital tools, the Council will make assessment more accessible, efficient and responsive to the people it serves.

In the end, ETC’s work is about Pakistan’s human capital. By measuring knowledge, skills and potential fairly, it helps the right people reach the right opportunities, and strengthens the culture of merit across our education system.

Section 03 · Chief Executive Officer

Building confidence
in assessment.

Message from the Chief Executive Officer, Education Testing Council

At ETC, we hold ourselves to one simple principle: every candidate deserves a test that is fair, transparent, secure and consistent. Whether a young person is applying to a law college, a scholarship holder is entering a PhD programme, or a professional is seeking a licence, they should be able to trust that the result reflects their own ability.

To make this possible, we have built our work on professional standards and operational discipline. Each question in our item bank is written by trained subject experts, reviewed by several committees, tried out on real candidates and analysed statistically before it is used to make decisions. After every live test, we study the results again, so that weak or unclear questions are corrected or removed. This cycle of checking and improving is what gives our scores their meaning.

Technology is an important partner in this effort. We use automated paper generation from a calibrated item bank, optical scanning for fast and accurate marking, and online services for registration and results. As we move forward, we plan a gradual shift to computer-based testing, stronger identity verification and smarter monitoring, always with careful safeguards for data protection and confidentiality.

Our responsibility does not end with conducting examinations. We work closely with institutions to help them identify talent and make sound decisions. We listen to candidates, answer their concerns and treat complaints seriously. We believe a testing body must earn trust through its actions: open processes, accountable governance, responsive service and a culture of quality.

I thank our Board of Governors, the Higher Education Commission, our subject experts and the entire ETC team for their commitment. Together, we will continue to grow ETC into an institution that Pakistan can rely on for fair and meaningful assessment.
