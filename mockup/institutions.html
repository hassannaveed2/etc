How an engagement works

Five steps from requirement to result

  1. 01

    Requirement study

    We meet you to understand the posts, programme or decision, the number of candidates and the timeline.

  2. 02

    Test design

    A blueprint and paper pattern are prepared and agreed with you.

  3. 03

    Item selection

    Calibrated items are drawn from the bank, or new items are written and reviewed.

  4. 04

    Administration

    The test is conducted at ETC centres under standard security protocols.

  5. 05

    Scoring & reporting

    Results are processed, checked and delivered with a result report and, where useful, analysis by learning outcome.
