      Quality & Methods

      How we protect every result

      A test score is only as good as the questions behind it. Every ETC item follows the same 13-step path before it can count towards a candidate’s score.

      1. 01

        Built to a blueprint

        A Table of Specifications fixes topics, number of questions and thinking levels in advance, using Bloom’s Taxonomy.

      2. 02

        Tested before it counts

        Items pass blind peer review and five quality committees, then are piloted on real students — 802 test batteries so far.

      3. 03

        Checked after every test

        Post-hoc analysis validates every key before results are released. Any faulty item is removed or credited to all candidates.

      Institutional Solutions

      Assessment services for institutions

      Universities, government departments, regulators and professional councils rely on ETC for standardized assessment, tailored to the decision they need to make.

      • Admissions

        Standardized tests that help universities and colleges select students fairly and on merit.

      • Bar Council licence

        The Law Graduate Assessment Test for law graduates seeking enrolment as advocates.

      • Scholarships

        Eligibility and merit tests for HEC and other national scholarship programmes.

      • Recruitment

        Job-related tests that help public bodies shortlist and select qualified candidates.

      • Promotions

        Assessments that support fair, evidence-based decisions on career progression.

      • Personality assessment

        Psychological and personality evaluations for selection and placement needs.
