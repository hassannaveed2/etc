Overview

Standardized, centralized and secure assessment

The Education Testing Council (ETC) is Pakistan’s public-sector national testing body, set up under the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Ordinance to provide standardized, centralized and secure assessment for education and professional opportunities.

Tests are designed to a fixed plan, delivered under the same conditions everywhere, and scored in the same way for every candidate — so results from different cities, sessions and test forms can be compared fairly.

Standardized
Same rules, same conditions, same scoring for every candidate.
Centralized
One national system for design, printing, delivery and results.
Secure
Controlled handling of papers, data and materials at every step.
Quality-driven
Every question reviewed, piloted and analysed before and after use.

Our journey

From conception to a national platform

  1. 2017

    Conceived

    ETC conceived as a public-sector testing body under the HEC Ordinance.

  2. Aug 2018

    Revamped

    The Commission’s 34th meeting (30–31 Aug 2018) approved a revamped ETC as an autonomous, self-sustaining entity.

  3. 31 Aug 2018

    Legal mandate

    The Supreme Court’s decision on legal education reforms assigned national law tests to HEC as the executing institution.

  4. 15 Jun 2022

    Gazette

    ETC formally notified in The Gazette of Pakistan.

  5. Today

    National platform

    103 test centres, a 230,000+ item bank and more than 1.85 million candidates tested since July 2021.

Legal mandate

Legal education reforms

On 31 August 2018, while considering the reports of the Legal Education Reform Committees in Constitution Petition No. 134/2012 and related applications, the Supreme Court of Pakistan decided that:

In simple terms

ETC carries out these responsibilities on behalf of HEC. LAT and LAW-GAT are now national gateways to legal education and to the legal profession.

  1. Para 8

    The Pakistan Bar Council (PBC) and the Higher Education Commission (HEC) are the key regulators responsible for supervising and enforcing professional standards in legal education.

  2. Para 15(1)

    A Law Graduate Assessment Test (LAW-GAT) for law graduates seeking enrolment to the Bar shall be held quarterly by HEC throughout Pakistan, using a question bank prepared for the test.

  3. Para 15(2)

    A Law Admission Test (LAT) for all law colleges in Pakistan shall be held twice a year by HEC.

Strategic roadmap

Where we are heading

ETC aims to become a fully digital, research-led and internationally certified national testing body that every candidate and institution in Pakistan can trust.

  • National assessment policy

    A Pakistan National Assessment Framework and data-driven policy input to higher-education reforms.

  • Standardized admissions

    Expand mandatory standardized admission tests such as USAT and HAT.

  • Digital transformation

    A gradual shift to nationwide computer-based testing (CBT).

  • Secure technology

    AI-assisted proctoring and biometric verification, with safeguards for data protection.

  • Advanced item bank

    A large-scale calibrated, psychometrically tagged and encrypted item bank.

  • International standards

    ISO certification for testing operations and certification for item writers.
