Test day

Your test-day journey

The same steps, the same rules and the same timing for every candidate, in every city.

Bring with you

  • Printed admit card (roll number slip)
  • Original CNIC or B-Form
  • Only the items permitted in your instructions

Mobile phones, smart watches and other electronic devices are not allowed.

  1. 1

    Registration

    Register and pay online, then receive your admit card with centre, date and time.

  2. 2

    Arrival and identity check

    You are verified against your admit card and identity document.

  3. 3

    Seating and instructions

    You are seated according to the seating plan and given standard instructions.

  4. 4

    Test administration

    Sealed papers are opened in front of candidates and timing is strictly followed.

  5. 5

    Collection and return

    Answer sheets are counted, sealed and returned securely to ETC.

  6. 6

    Scanning and analysis

    Answer sheets are scanned, scored and analysed, including post-hoc item analysis.

  7. 7

    Results

    Results are published online and shared with client institutions.

Transparency

Our commitment to candidates

What you can expect from ETC

  • Clear, advance information about the test purpose, format, syllabus, timing, fee and rules.
  • Equal treatment under the same conditions, wherever and whenever the test is taken.
  • Questions that have been reviewed for accuracy and fairness, and statistically checked.
  • Reasonable arrangements for candidates with disabilities or special needs, on request.
  • Protection of personal data and confidentiality of results.
  • Timely results, and a clear route to raise questions or complaints.
  • A fair hearing before any decision on a case of alleged unfair means.

What ETC expects from you

  • Read all instructions and follow the test rules.
  • Provide accurate personal information and carry valid identification.
  • Arrive on time and bring only permitted items.
  • Do your own work honestly; any form of cheating or impersonation leads to disciplinary action.
  • Keep the content of the test confidential.

Unfair means

Cases of alleged unfair means are heard by ETC’s independent Quality Assurance Department through a set, documented procedure. The candidate is always heard before a decision is made.

Read the unfair means policy

Accessibility & special accommodations

ETC provides reasonable arrangements for candidates with disabilities or special needs. Request them during registration with supporting documents, so arrangements can be confirmed before your test.
